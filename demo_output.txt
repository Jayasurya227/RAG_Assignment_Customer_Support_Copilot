Routing Decision: Indexes: [product_index, ticket_index] | Reason: Rule-based classification matched keywords for ticket_index, product_index.

If the problem persists within 48 hours of delivery, it can be reported as defective.

--- Q4 (Order Guide & In-Flight Address Change) ---
Question: A customer wants to change the address after the order has shipped. Can they do it, and what should support tell them?
Routing Decision: Indexes: [order_index] | Reason: Rule-based classification matched keywords for order_index.
Answer: According to official order guidelines (OG-002), delivery addresses cannot be changed once the order has already shipped. As shown in resolved case TCK-1004, the customer refused delivery of their shipped order, and a full refund was processed once the return package reached the fulfillment center.
Sources: OG-002, TCK-1004

--- Q5 (Exact Code Matching: Policy RR-004) ---
Question: What does policy RR-004 say?
Routing Decision: Indexes: [policy_index] | Reason: Rule-based classification matched keywords for policy_index.
Answer: Policy RR-004 defines refund timelines across payment methods after returned items pass quality inspection. UPI and wallet refunds are processed within 1 to 3 business days. Cash on Delivery (COD) refunds are credited to the customer's bank account within 5 to 7 business days after bank details are submitted on the Orders page.
